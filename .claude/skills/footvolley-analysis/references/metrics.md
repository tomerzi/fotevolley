# Metric definitions and checks

Unless prefixed below, metric CSVs are in `data/analysis/` on the primary coaching branch. Check their scripts in `scripts/analysis/`.

## Position and movement

- **Coverage and heatmaps.** Use `coverage_match_v3.csv`, `coverage_rallies_v3.csv`, `coverage_heatmaps_v3.png` and `coverage_reach.json` where applicable. Read each producer to identify observed occupancy versus modelled reachable area. For custom occupancy use unique player/frame rows from `player_positions_v3.csv` inside rallies, measured ground points, declared grid or smoothing, metres squared and seconds. Report measured coverage, area containing 50%/90% of dwell time if available, and share outside lines. For team coverage take a spatial union. Record parameters and use the same scale for player comparisons.
- **Phase maps and A/B roles.** Use `phase_heatmaps.json`, `phase_heatmaps_summary.csv`, `phase_heatmaps_pairs.csv` and `phase_heatmaps.png`. In the existing model serve is the serving team's pre-reception interval, reception includes incoming serves and attacks, build is own possession before attack, attack is the post-attack interval, and defence is opponent build before attack. Split serve reception from defence against attacks when the request calls for it. Audit inferred pre-first-contact roles against visible serves and action labels, since broadcast starts late. Compare A/B by matched team, phase and set using normalized dwell distributions, overlap, lateral share and median or mean distance from net. Show observed seconds. Derive stable roles only if patterns persist across rallies/sets.
- **Partner spacing.** Use `team_spacing_v3.csv`, `formation_frames.csv`, `formation_points.csv` and `conceded_partner_distance.csv`. Pair teammates at the same event and frame, then compute Euclidean distance and optional cross-court and depth components. Use median and IQR by phase, set and outcome. Identify whether the event is an attack, own-error touch or reliable landing. Filter missing and weak ground points.
- **Positions when winning and conceding.** Combine `point_positions.csv` / `point_positions_summary.csv` with the reviewed ending and winner. Distinguish player's ground position at contact or the ending frame, contact location of the ball and terminal landing. Give zones relative to each team's net and separate point counts from dwell-normalized zone comparisons.
- **Recovery to base.** Use `return_to_base.csv`, `return_to_base_bases.csv`, `return_to_base_summary.csv`. Current rule uses each player's mean measured rally position in own-view coordinates and first entry within 1 m after their attack or set. A touch already within 1 m returns in 0 s. End of rally, next own touch or a gap that prevents determining the return may censor observations. Report events, returns, censored counts, and median/IQR among observed returns with that label. Compare optional per-set bases explicitly. Verify event rows against current in-play corrections.

## Contacts and outcomes

- **Touches by body part.** Count distinct reviewed in-play player touch IDs from `data/contacts/touches_master.csv` plus owner corrections. Group by player, body part, action and set as requested. Include unknown body parts explicitly. Separate serve from possession ordinal where identifiable, and exclude ball ground/net events and dead-ball contacts.
- **Winning body part and error chain.** Use `point_touches.csv` with owner decisions, `touch_outcomes.csv` and reviewed point ends. Verify actual winning contact and its part, and report points won by opponent errors separately. For losses show winner, ending type, last overall touch, last losing-side touch, body part, action, and its touch number 1–3 within its possession. Include the available preceding sequence with clip/frame references. Reset possession at a verified crossing or team change when supported. Same-team touches separated by a crossing and missing opponent contact cannot establish a certain ordinal. Blank or disputed body parts and ordinals stay unknown; a confirmed player name by itself cannot validate a contact inferred at a different moment.
- **Touch success and reception stability.** Start with `touch_outcomes.csv` and recompute denominators from per-touch rows. The repo's basic success is retaining or advancing play rather than losing on that touch. Audit final/unseen touches before scoring. Report success / eligible touches, unknown outcomes, and the stricter rate of receptions whose possession reaches an attack. Compare techniques by player and incoming serve versus attack, with Wilson intervals or another stated uncertainty method. Treat small groups as provisional and avoid ranking tied or weakly separated techniques. Point-win rate may be shown separately.
- **Set quality and air time.** `set_quality.csv` includes receptions passed to a partner, so filter `action=set` for set-specific metrics. Use the next confirmed in-play touch in the same rally, and distinguish partner attack, other continuation, and failed or unknown sets. Compute elapsed event time or frame delta / FPS on a continuous video segment. Include sample size and median/IQR of time to next contact, apex and its uncertainty from `ball_height_v2.csv`, partner displacement, and share setting up an attack. Keep failed sets in the quality denominator even when air time is missing. Treat flight duration alone as descriptive.
- **Terminal ball landings.** Map measured or reviewed `landed_in` / `landed_out` positions using `data/points/point_end_verified.csv` and owner marks. Plot a crossed boundary as a separate edge marker if only the line crossed is known. Report net, fault, unseen and missing-coordinate points as separate counts. Use `point_landings.csv` only as a labeled automatic estimate when useful. Reconcile all ending categories against the rally count.

## Technique and orientation

- **Chest action body angles.** Join `touch_body.csv` and `chest_profile.csv` by touch ID after checking frames and player. Filter chest and split reception, set and attack where supported. Report joint angles (knees, hips, elbows, upper arm, neck, torso lean) in degrees only where the corresponding `*_ok` and left/right identity are reliable. State angle convention and sample count per measure. These are one-camera image-plane estimates. Stance width, incoming ball height and descent give context.
- **Stable versus arriving in motion.** Use `stance_label` from `touch_body.csv` for owner-labeled stable/moving/air categories. On the inspected tip, the best automatic rule scored 52% on the 69 labeled touches. Report the labeled sample and missing share. For unlabelled rows, report continuous `approach_speed` and `speed_at_touch` from `touch_speed_v3.csv` as motion estimates. Keep automatic foot placement and jump classifications exploratory.
- **Body direction.** Use `body_orientation_facing.csv`, `body_orientation_0_15585.csv` and `touch_body.csv` with their producers. Camera-facing category is distinct from orientation relative to the net, torso from head/gaze, and a shoulder axis from a signed forward direction. Prefer manual labels where available. Respect `orientation_ok`, frame and body-part coverage, occlusion and uncertainty; report direction as unknown when not identifiable. Use circular statistics for directional angles, 180° axial handling for symmetric axes, and normalize to the team's frame before comparing sides.

## Report checks

For each metric cite the source SHA and path, definition, unit, coverage and limitations. Check court extents, side flips, nonnegative durations, per-frame uniqueness, and totals after grouping. Inspect cases such as point 2 dead ball, point 27 hidden contact, point 49 scoreboard correction and point 56 owner-added touch. If a derived metric disagrees with an owner label, trace the discrepancy to its fields and list affected rallies. Compare opportunities as well as percentages, and tie any coaching inference to the observable sequence and its sample.
